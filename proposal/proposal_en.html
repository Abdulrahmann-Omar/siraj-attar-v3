Media plan · 90 days

The first 90 days: test, learn, then scale what sells

Days 1–30 · Learn

15 Nov – 14 Dec
  • Tracking checked end to end before any spend
  • Launch on 15 Nov: 16 test ads, 4 ideas in two registers and two formats
  • Peak 26–30 Nov: salary, White Friday and the school break, then the first read-out

Days 31–60 · Optimise

15 Dec – 13 Jan
  • Weakest half of the ads cut; retargeting live
  • Winter set for al-Murabba'aniyah and the Eid fabric message for the tailors' peak
  • Salary burst 27–31 Dec; mid-year break from 8 Jan

Days 61–90 · Scale

14 Jan – 12 Feb
  • Winners scaled by 20–30% every two to three days
  • Wool and heavy shemagh; salary burst 27–31 Jan
  • 90-day review on 12 Feb; the Ramadan and Eid flight starts 13 Feb

Creative testing

  • فصحى against Saudi on the same design: CTR, conversion rate and cost per customer, per platform
  • Hypothesis: Saudi leads on Snapchat and TikTok, فصحى holds on Google and X
  • 3 hooks per hero product; after 72 hours the budget moves to the winner
  • After 3,000 to 5,000 impressions, the bottom quarter is paused

What we measure

  • Primary: cost per new customer, kept under break-even (order value × margin)
  • Example: a 300 order at 50% margin breaks even at 150; the target is 100 or less
  • Secondary: return on ad spend of 3× or more, add-to-cart rate, click-through rate
  • A one-page report every week, a review every month

Tracking on Salla, before any spend

  • Snapchat: Snap Pixel with the Conversions API
  • TikTok: Events API with the catalogue
  • Meta: pixel and Conversions API, daily catalogue sync
  • Google: Merchant Center with VAT-inclusive prices, GA4 through your Tag Manager
  • One event ID per order, so nothing is counted twice; Apple Pay, Tabby and Tamara checkouts tested
