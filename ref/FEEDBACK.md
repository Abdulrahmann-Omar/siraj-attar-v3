# Client-side feedback on the previous proposal and posters (verbatim summary)

General
1. Customer branding is part of the presentation: the customer's logo must stand out on the first page and feel
   intentionally showcased, not pasted. The opening slide must say "made specifically for you".
2. Images are critical. Bad crops, wrong transparencies, poor contrast against the background, or unfinished-looking
   images kill the pitch. Every image must be presentation-ready.
3. Local market awareness in the details: "ر.س" reads as not knowing the market. Use the official Saudi Riyal symbol.
4. The Arabic font INSIDE the images was a wise choice and works well. The Arabic font used in the PRESENTATION itself
   should be reconsidered: pick one that matches the quality and character of the visuals.

Specific
1. "80+ years of craft. Every post should show it." Excellent: short, memorable, ties product to heritage. Keep it.
2. Catalogue posters look good and polished. Keep the approach.
3. Scenario imagery (product in different scenarios) is an excellent idea; every image must be finished to the same
   standard as the concept.

Main question: HOW?
The problem and the beautiful end result are communicated well, but HOW the result is achieved is missing.
Add a simple, clear hook explaining how the product makes that result possible.
"Not clear the before and after at all, you have to dig deep to get it." -> before/after must be instantly readable.
"Either رأس or لرجلينك. This is a mix of عامي وفصحى." -> never mix registers in one piece of copy.
